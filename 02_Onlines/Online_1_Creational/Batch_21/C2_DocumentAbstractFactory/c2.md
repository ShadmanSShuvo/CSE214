### CSE 214 - Creational Pattern Online

**C2: Multi-Style Document Generation**

You are designing a system to generate documents in different styles. The system needs to support the creation of letters and resumes. The document creator you are implementing has two modes: one for professional and formal use and the other for informal usage.

The client can select its preferred mode. After that, they can create letters or resumes from that mode with their preferred style (formal/informal). The classes that represent document creators must have functions that return objects of letters or resumes appropriately.

**Pattern:** Abstract Factory Pattern
